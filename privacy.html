Autopublisher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Autopublisher (the “Service”), operated by Alvaipillai digital solution, handles data. The Service is a personal tool: it only processes data of the accounts its operator connects.

1. Data we access

When a TikTok account is connected through Login Kit, the Service receives, with the account owner's consent:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), profile details and account statistics;
  • the list of the account's public videos, to show the status of published posts;
  • access tokens that allow the Service to publish videos to that account.

The Service also processes the videos and captions that the operator chooses to publish.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to publish the operator's videos to their own accounts, to schedule those publications, and to show the operator the result (published, failed, link to the post). We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Where it is stored

Access tokens, videos and publication history are stored on the operator's own self-hosted server. They are sent only to the platform concerned (for example TikTok) when a video is published. To draft captions, a few still images taken from the operator's own videos may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API; no TikTok account data is included.

4. Retention

Account data and tokens are kept while the account remains connected and are deleted when it is disconnected from the Service. You can also revoke access at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, under the applicable data protection laws.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers of its own. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Contact

For any privacy question or request, open an issue on our GitHub repository.
