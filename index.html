Autopublisher

Publish your own videos everywhere, automatically

Autopublisher is a personal tool operated by Alvaipillai digital solution. It publishes the operator's own videos to the operator's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook) through a self-hosted instance of the open-source scheduler Postiz.

How it works

  1. The operator drops a video into a folder or a local web page.
  2. The video is converted to the formats expected by each platform.
  3. A title and captions are taken from a text file, or drafted automatically.
  4. The video is published, or scheduled, on the operator's connected accounts.

TikTok integration

Autopublisher uses Login Kit so the operator can connect their TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to publish their videos directly to that account. It is not offered to the public: only the operator's own accounts are connected.
